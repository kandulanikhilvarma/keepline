Today, this month, this year, or your own dates. Keep a daily reminder active with “Always”.

Keep it in sight

Add the rectangular widget. Rotate active lines, or pin one that needs your attention.

The widget follows an iOS timeline. Choose about 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 3 hours between lines. iOS can delay updates to save battery. It cannot change the line on every unlock.

Made for iPhone.
Ready for your first build.

The native app and widget are open source. This release has no App Store or TestFlight download yet.

Open the iPhone setup guide
  1. Build on a Mac with Xcode.

    Generate the included Xcode project. Select your Apple team and configure the App Group for both targets.

  2. Install and save your first line.

    Run the app on your iPhone. Apple signing and the App Group must be active for the shared widget data.

  3. Add Keepline to your home screen.

    Touch and hold the home screen. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Keepline and choose the rectangle.

A private place
for personal words.

The iPhone app stores your lines on your device. No account. No remote database. No analytics or advertising.

You can export your library as a backup and import it later. Backups contain plain text, so keep them somewhere private.

Your widget is visible to anyone who can see your screen. The site stores its preview draft in this browser only. The hosting provider receives normal web requests.
